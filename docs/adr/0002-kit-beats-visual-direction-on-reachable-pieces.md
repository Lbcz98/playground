# The kit's rules beat the visual direction for pieces the remote reaches

A visual direction may ask to avoid outlined boxes while the kit requires a resting outline on every piece the remote control can reach, and the chosen piece inverted. We decided the kit wins on those pieces, so a direction never removes them. The direction instead limits their weight: the chosen piece is small and low, and never a bigger mass of the strongest colour than the focal group.

Considered options: let the direction override the kit per exploration (rejected: the focus and selection language would differ between screens).
