# Direction: repertoire

Applies when: the designer gave no reference, or the art director needs a language to name. This is DIRECAO §4.

Each entry: what it is, its signature, what it does for a card, the trap, and how the kit can carry it. Drawing limits apply: one accent, three weights, no drop shadows, no new gradients (`color.md`, `type.md`, `elevation.md`). Reference works are a place to look; the idea is taken, not the look.

1. **Quiet corner.** One small title pulled into a corner, a single large empty field, an accent used once. Signature: void-to-text of roughly 8:1 or more. For: states that should feel calm. Trap: empty without a reason. Kit: `grupo-por-espaco`, `ancora-e-apoio`, one muted caption.
2. **Key-art with a calm band.** A large image, text only on its calm area, a scrim where needed. For: content where mood sells. Trap: text over the busy part; two focal points. Kit: the card scrim, the image variant of the Content Card.
3. **Swiss / grid typographic.** Type as the image: scale jumps, strict left axis, numerals as graphic. For: data that deserves to be looked at. Trap: coldness; fix with one warm accent and generous leading. Kit: `.text-*` steps, tabular numerals.
4. **Poster type.** One huge word or number cropped by the edge or stacked, the rest tiny. For: a single message. Trap: legibility when cropped; keep the cropped part decorative. Kit: a large literal size is allowed only if said in the report.
5. **Data as landscape.** The data's own shape is the picture; numbers sit on it as labels. For: continuous data. Trap: the chart becomes the loudest thing. Kit: charts are not icons; the focal group keeps its weight.
6. **Ma (negative space).** An object sits off-centre with a lot of room on one side. For: calm, slow reading. Trap: confusing space with absence of content. Kit: the largest spacing steps, one piece per area.
7. **Instrument / product design.** A few clear controls, one numeral display, a tactile label system. For: controls on a TV. Trap: skeuomorphism; take the layout logic, not the knobs. Kit: segmented controls, the kit ring.
8. **Mood gradient.** A single field of graded colour that stands for a state. For: states with a feeling. Trap: gradient as decoration; it must map to the data. Kit: only the gradients already in the kit.
9. **Departure board / flip numerals.** Mechanical numerals, fixed slots, one change at a time. For: values that change on a key. Trap: nostalgia without function. Kit: fixed-width numerals, a short transition that respects reduced motion.
10. **Album cover minimalism.** A fixed template; the variable is one image or one word. For: sets of similar cards. Trap: monotony; vary one thing only. Kit: `esqueleto-fixo`.
11. **Magazine spread.** A big type moment, a caption, a hairline, an image. For: editorial cards. Trap: too many typographic voices. Kit: two weights, one scale jump.
12. **Dark-room reading.** Dark field, one light source, glows kept low, text brighter than its field by a clear margin. For: everything on this platform. Trap: pure black plus pure white. Kit: neutral steps, `elevation.md`.

The director may name a language that is not in the list when the designer's reference demands it; it then adds the entry here (same five parts) after the designer confirms.

No reference at all: pick two entries that fit the Intenção and the Foco; they stand in for the reference, and the direction says so.
