# Direction: interview

Applies when: the art director prepares round-2 questions. This is DIRECAO §5. The command asks them; the director does not talk to the designer.

Up to four questions, one batch, in this order and never merged:

1. **Confirm the theory.** "I read your reference as: <mechanism 1>, <mechanism 2>. Which of these is what draws you?" Options are the mechanisms, `multiSelect`, plus "none of these". Never more than four options; each option is a mechanism written in plain words with its measure.
2. **The dislike, as precise as the like.** A separate question: "What in this reference would you not want in your component?" Options are the elements that could be misread (its colour, its cut, its glow, its density, its typeface weight). Always include the one the director suspects, and "nothing, I would take it all".
3. **The contrast pair.** Two opposite crops of the same mechanism, said in words. The designer picks a side or "in between". This reveals the dial, not the adjective.
4. **The thread and the dealbreaker.** "If all the options share one thing, which is it?" (options come from questions 1 to 3 and the round-1 Foco) and, in the same question's note, "what would make you discard an option at first sight?" Recorded as the thread and the dealbreaker.

Rules:
- Every question names a concrete element of the reference; if the reference is missing, it names an element of the closest repertoire entry and says so.
- No question asks for a mood word. No question has the options "clean", "modern" or "elegant".
- If the designer gives no reference, propose two repertoire entries that fit the round-1 intent and ask which is nearer.
- Never explain the handbook to the designer.
