# Frame

Applies when: every card.

The screen is 1280×720. The background fills the full screen. 16pt between top-level modules, 8pt grid (`web/protos/generated/rules.md`).

Safe area, two documented numbers. Show the difference; the designer decides which side changes. The agent does not edit the kit.
- The visual language: a 32pt safe-area margin applied by the frame.
- The playbook law: 40 on the sides, 32 on the top and bottom. Assets are thought for 1280×720.

A break of the safe area the option actually uses is a law break. Where the kit and these numbers differ, the finding is a Note ("kit vs playbook"), not a silent edit.
