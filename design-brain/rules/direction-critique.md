# Direction: critique

Applies when: the art director writes `direcao.md`, and an explorer checks its own PNG against the brief before reporting. This is DIRECAO §7.

Input: the brief's Direção visual and the PNG of each option (plus the keys' PNGs when the shot script printed them). Output: one verdict per option, written to `docs/explorations/<slug>/direcao.md`:

`Opção N: <Tem a cara | Quase | Genérica>. Levar: <ok or what is missing, with the measure>. Evitar: <ok or what crept in>. Teste de fidelidade: <sim/não>. Genérico a olho: <the stock moves found, from the list below>. Um ajuste: <one move | none: ready>.`

**Stock moves (the "generic" tells).** Name any that appear: centred stack of icon, number and label; rows of equal weight; every piece the same size within 1.5×; an accent colour used for decoration; a gradient with no data behind it; an illustration that could belong to any app; chrome that outweighs content; text pairs with no scale jump; empty space that is leftover rather than placed; a control that is louder than the thing it controls; options that differ only in layout.

Rules: a verdict is evidence from the PNG, never taste. "Quase" always says the one move that would make it "Tem a cara". The critic never edits and never says an option is "good": it says whether it is *this* direction. `Um ajuste: none: ready` means the option already holds the direction, and only when the brief has a Direção visual block (Tom, Fio condutor, Levar, Evitar, Teste de fidelidade). Without that block, ready is not a verdict.

The command applies one adjustment, once, and only to the options listed on the `Aplicar:` line. It does not close an option on `none: ready` while the judge still has a Critical or a Warning on it.

**Aplicar.** After the verdicts, one line: `Aplicar: <n>` or `Aplicar: <n>, <m>` or `Aplicar: nenhuma`. Pick the one or two options that best carry the thread and still have a single adjustment worth making. The others keep their verdict and are not sent back to a writer. `Aplicar: nenhuma` when every picked candidate is already `none: ready` and you would not spend a pass on it.
