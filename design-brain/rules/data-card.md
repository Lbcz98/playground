# Data card

Applies when: the brief or the direction is about data (a number, a temperature, a chart, weather, a metric, an axis, a status), or the direction names `tipografia-de-detalhe`.

1. Data about the same subject stay together: a participant's name, age and estalecas read as one group. Metadata that belong together (rating, genre, duration) form one group, close to the title, or sit on the image on purpose. A piece that sits in the layout without a group is loose.
2. A selector shows only the information of the chosen section. Information that follows the focus (the viewer sees the data without pressing Enter) is a strength. Flag it only when the chosen state and the information shown contradict each other without a visible cue.
3. A status or number is never larger or stronger than the title. The content is the protagonist.
4. Order of sacrifice on a video card (home, "sell" fast): image, title, social proof (rating), genre. Description and action go to the detail page; the card is the button. The order depends on the screen's goal.
5. A chart that is broken or hard to read is a failed picture. In a chart the cells only map the focus: the focus is invisible and the dot or bar that reacts is the signal (`focus.md`). The active mark clearly differs from the others (at least 3:1, or a clear size change).
6. A decorative data display not tied to the intent is a failure.
7. Changing numbers use tabular numerals. The type detail is `conceitos/tipografia-de-detalhe`.

Don't: related data split apart; a status louder than the title; a selector that leaves the other section's information on screen; a chart that cannot be read.
