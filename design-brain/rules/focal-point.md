# Focal point

Applies when: every card. The recipe of the focal group is `conceitos/ponto-focal` (already in the pack). This file holds the hierarchy rules that recipe does not.

The content is the protagonist, not the component. When in doubt, remove. What stays is extremely visual. A screen says a lot with little.

1. One anchor datum per card, at least 2× the support text (3× for a short number). The size recipe is `conceitos/ancora-e-apoio`. *Why: without an anchor the eye does not know where to start; with two they compete.*
2. Hierarchy comes from size and weight, not from colour. *Why: colour is reserved for meaning (`color.md`).*
3. Label above the value, smaller and softer. Unit and delta share the value's baseline at about half its size. The one exception is the degree sign `°` on a display numeral: it is top-aligned to the digits at about half the size; every other unit (mph, %, km/h) stays on the baseline. *Why: the number is what the person came for, and the raised degree is how a temperature reads.*
4. A status number is never louder than the title. The title says what this is; the status only qualifies it. The status line, the time left and the title belong to one group.
5. In a row of equal items, the chosen one stands out by inverted background, not by larger text. *Why: equal labels stay comparable.*
6. Judge intent, not taste: does it serve the brief, fit where it lives, bring something new to the system?

Don't: several elements fighting for emphasis, or a second group with the same weight as the focal group.
