# Check: frame

Applies when: every option that is judged. Norma: `frame.md`.

A card that leaves the 1280×720 frame or the frame's 32pt screen margin is Critical. 40px is the card radius, not a padding, an inset, or a safe area. Do not report a side margin of 40px, and do not leave the radius and the padding for the designer to pick between.
