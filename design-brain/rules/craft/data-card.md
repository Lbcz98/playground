# Check: data card

Applies when: the option shows data. Norma: `data-card.md`.

From the picture: a chart that is broken or hard to read, a place name lost in the layout, or a group of data split from its selector is a Warning, and Critical when the graphic cannot be read.

A status louder than the title is a Warning. Related data split apart is a Warning. A selector that shows the wrong section's information is a Warning. A decorative display with no intent is a Warning.
