# Severity

Applies when: every playbook judgment.

- **Critical:** a law is broken. ↑/↓ consumed in a card, more than one interaction pattern, a web-only interaction, pure white or black, grey text on black, a decision-carrying text under the body-sm floor or any text at the caption size, safe-area violation, hover or animation needed to work, items stacked vertically under ←/→, more than 4 repeated items without the designer's decision, a focus ring with no room, an SVG icon. Shown first; the option is marked "breaks a law". The designer still decides.
- **Warning:** a token or scale value is off, or a principle is weak (hierarchy, proximity, respiro, too many elements, a second accent, a focusable piece with no sign at rest, a false affordance, related data split apart, a status louder than the title).
- **Note:** a preference was bent, a deviation was justified in the rationale, or `.text-footnote-*` on a secondary label that carries no decision. At footnote size on text that carries a decision it is Critical.
- **Note, "kit vs playbook":** the kit offers no compliant token. The designer decides which side changes. The judge never edits the kit.

When the option chose a banned token while a compliant one exists, it is Critical, and the finding names the compliant token.

A gradient outside the allowed list, a drop shadow, a second accent, extra-bold, or a scrim with no text on the image is a Warning; a drop shadow or an invented gradient is Critical when the option chose it while the kit has a compliant surface.

Every finding names the element, the rule or token, the level and the documented value. The judge does not pick a winner.
