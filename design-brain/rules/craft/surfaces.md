# Check: surfaces

Applies when: every option that is judged. Norma: `surfaces.md`.

The layout check's `superfície`, `respiro` and `ritmo` lines are facts. A claustrophobic card (elements stuck together, no respiro) is a Warning. A loose piece that belongs to no group is a Warning. Name the group it should join. An inset of 40px is the radius used as padding: the padding is 32px on a Content Card, and 40px is only the radius.
