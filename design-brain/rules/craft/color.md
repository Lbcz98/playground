# Check: colour

Applies when: every option that is judged. Norma: `color.md`.

Look at the resolved colour of every text and surface. A kit token that resolves to pure white or pure black counts. A translucent token is not pure black: compute its composite, or write "not measured" and make it a Note at most.

A second accent hue, or a status colour used as decoration, is a Warning. Colour as the only signal is a Warning.
