# Check: repeated items

Applies when: the option has a row of repeated items. Norma: `repeated-items.md`.

Count the items and name them. More than 4 without `data-items-ok` is Critical. The fix is to reframe, not to shrink.

If the count caused small text or a tight ring, write one finding. A plainer option than the one it replaces is a Warning ("Reduce items, not richness"), or Critical when the graphic cannot be read.
