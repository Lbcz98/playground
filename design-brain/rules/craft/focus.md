# Check: focus

Applies when: the option is interactive or draws a focus state. Norma: `focus.md`.

The layout check's `anel` line is the measure. A ring that touches content is Critical; the fix is fewer items or less content, never a thinner ring.

A button-like piece the remote reaches with no resting outline, while it is not focused or selected, is a Warning. A filled selected piece is exempt, and so is a toggle or segmented selector. In a chart, a missing ring is not an escalation trigger when the active mark clearly differs.

Focus shown by scale, movement or a colour change is Critical.
