# Check: type

Applies when: the option has text. Norma: `type.md`.

Measure the smallest font size inside the card. Decision-carrying text under the body-sm floor, or any text at the caption size, is Critical. Footnote on a secondary label that carries no decision is a Note.

Warning, unless the designer said otherwise: a title that breaks so a short function word starts the next line, or a river of empty space; a capital on every word of a title or label that is not a name.

Cause before symptom: if the item count caused the small text, one finding only (`repeated-items.md`).
