# Check: focal point

Applies when: every option that is judged. Norma: `focal-point.md` and `conceitos/ponto-focal`.

Read the brief's Foco and the PNG. The group it names must be read first, set apart by at least 3 levers, while the rest recedes. Two groups of equal weight, or the focal group lost among equals, is a Warning (a Critical when nothing is read first). The fix is to remove or mute, never to decorate.
